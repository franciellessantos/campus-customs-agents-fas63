3 · Three ways to simplify the agents' work

  1. Route the first call in code by ticket type. customer_order and price_override → Inventory; rent_notice → Facilities. The Boss would make only the final call. Ticket 103 shows why: the Boss's free choice sent the work to Accounting first, which caused the back-and-forth and an extra Accounting call. A fixed first step would have followed the Expected route, likely at about half the 54,905 tokens.
  2. Give each ticket one "context" tool and richer tool outputs. Every agent started with get_ticket (see the tool columns in the Actual tables), and Inventory had to ask Accounting for invoice 501's id. A single get_ticket_context(ticket_id) could return the ticket, stock and margin, the linked invoice and vendor block, the lease, cash and pending approvals. Having list_vendors return open invoice ids would remove a whole delegation hop.
  3. Share a facts ledger and ban sending work back. Today only sending work to the Boss is blocked, so Accounting and Inventory could pass the same question back and forth on #103. If the run kept a shared list of confirmed facts (stock 8, invoice 501 open, approval #1 pending, cash $3,400) and any reply to whoever asked were blocked, each agent would build on the last instead of re-asking. This also covers the 102 gap: the pending $840 would be on the ledger, so Facilities would see that paying rent too leaves $160.

4 · Three new problems these agents can solve

  1. Choosing which payment to approve when cash is tight. Checking is $2,560 with $8 payable. Rent ($2,400, due 2026-09-02) and the 12-hoodie order ($264) are both still pending, and doing both would cost $2,664, more than the $2,552 free. Accounting and Facilities already have every tool they need: get_cash_balance, get_lease_and_cash and list_payments_and_approvals. They can rank rent first (legal deadline, keeps the shop open), explain that the hoodie order would then be refused for insufficient funds, and suggest a smaller order. The dashboard banner already enforces the limit.
  2. Restocking Crest Mugs from a different vendor. CC-MUG-CREST has 0 on hand. Elm City Gifts (mugs and small goods, 3-day lead time) has no open invoice, so unlike Bulldog Print Co it isn't blocked. Inventory can confirm the stockout with check_stock, pick the right vendor with list_vendors, and draft a costed order with draft_purchase_order ($3.50 per mug, arriving 2026-09-03). It would land as an account payable for Fran, and no payment approval is needed.
  3. A margin-safe bulk quote that can be filled today. If a club asked for 30 Yale Caps (CC-HAT-BLUE), the shop has 40 in stock at $6 cost and $22 list. Inventory confirms the count, Accounting works out a discount that keeps the price above $6 (for example, 15% off = $18.70 each, $561 total, $381 margin), and Customer Service drafts the offer. This is the ticket-103 pattern without the restock blocker, so the team could make a real offer. It works best once a discount policy is added to the prompts.

5 · Three new problems these agents couldn't solve

  1. Selling something and collecting the money. Even if Yale AI Club accepts, nobody can ship the 8 hoodies on hand or charge for them. No tool lowers inventory.qty, there is no sales or orders table, and by design "money only goes out": cash_accounts can only drop. Refunds, deposits and paid orders all need new tables, inflow tools and a human-approved way to receive money.
  2. Doing anything in the real world. The team can't send Tauhid Zaman's approved draft, place order #1 with Bulldog Print Co, or call Elm City Properties about rent. Drafts are never sent by rule, and the database has no emails or phone numbers; Customer Service had to address drafts by name only. Even after Fran approves, a person still has to carry out each action.
  3. Judgment calls with no data behind them, and time moving forward. The team couldn't set a bulk-discount policy (103 ended with "no authorized bulk-discount policy was identified"), forecast back-to-school demand, or find a faster vendor than Bulldog Print Co's 5-day lead time, because none of that is in the data. Time is also frozen at desk.date_today = 2026-08-31. The agents can't follow up when the tee arrives on 2026-09-05, notice that rent became overdue on 2026-09-03, or pay the $8 account payable on its due date. They react to tickets but can't keep watch over time.
